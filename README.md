# Elías Elíseo Ramírez Calderón

**Economist | Financial Intermediation · Macro-Finance · Banking · Financial Stability**

Economist interested in how financial institutions adjust to macro-financial shocks and regulatory interventions, and how these responses affect credit allocation and economic activity in emerging economies.

My empirical work focuses on banking, financial regulation, and macro-financial dynamics using time-series and panel-data methods.

## Selected Research Projects

### Directed Credit, Financial Regulation, and Macroeconomic Activity in Bolivia

Empirical study of how directed-credit policies and financial regulation relate to credit allocation and real economic activity in Bolivia using monthly macro-financial data.

**Methods:** ARDL · ECM · VAR · HAC inference · Time-series diagnostics

*Repository and reproducible research report in preparation.*

### Bank Liquidity and Funding Fragility in Bolivia

Empirical research on liquidity and funding vulnerability in the Bolivian banking system, examining bank balance-sheet conditions and their interaction with macro-financial developments.

*[Repository and reproducible research report in preparation](https://eliasramirezc.github.io/bank-liquidity-funding-fragility-bolivia/).*
